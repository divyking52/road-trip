08 · Pre-Departure Briefing

Questions Before You Turn the Key

Everything first-time self-drivers, solo travellers, and private groups ask before joining a Meridian convoy.

Ask a Road Captain
01 How do I book a road trip?
Pick any curated expedition from our Road Trips showcase or send your preferred dates via the Enquiry Form below. Within 24 hours, a dedicated Road Captain confirms open seats, shares the full day-by-day route dossier, and provides a transparent booking link to lock your spot with a refundable booking deposit.
02 Are vehicles included in the package?
Both formats are available on every departure. You can drive your own well-maintained car/SUV (our team provides a pre-trip checklist), opt for a Meridian-prepped 4x4 rental SUV added to your package, or book a passenger seat inside a shared lead or companion vehicle.
03 Can solo travellers join?
Absolutely — nearly 40% of our convoy guests join solo. We pair solo travellers comfortably into shared 4x4 vehicles (or let you drive your own car in convoy), match room preferences transparently, and build warm small-group dynamics from the very first briefing dinner.
04 Are road trips suitable for beginners?
Yes. Each package displays a clear difficulty rating (Easy, Leisurely, Moderate, Challenging, or Expedition). Beginners thrive on our Coastal, Weekend, and Desert routes, while high-altitude Himalayan traverses include pre-trip hill-driving coaching and constant VHF radio guidance from our lead captain.
05 What happens if the weather changes?
Mountain and coastal weather moves fast, which is why every Meridian itinerary carries pre-vetted contingency routes and backup boutique stays. Our 24/7 operations desk tracks live pass advisories and reroutes the convoy smoothly without compromising safety or comfort.
06 What is included in the package price?
Standard packages include handpicked boutique accommodation, curated breakfast and dinner tables, lead and sweep Road Captains, in-car VHF walkie-talkies, inner-line/forest permits, mechanical breakdown support, medical & oxygen backup on mountain routes, and printed route books. Fuel, tolls, and optional SUV rental are transparently itemised.
07 What is the cancellation policy?
In our demo booking policy, cancellations 30+ days before departure receive a 90% refund or a 100% credit toward any future expedition within 12 months. Cancellations 15–29 days prior carry a 50% refund or free date transfer subject to seat availability. Replace these demo terms with your official policy prior to launch.
08 Do you organise private group trips?
Yes. We craft bespoke private convoys for families, milestone celebrations, photography clubs, classic-car collectives, and executive leadership offsites — customised down to vehicle fleet, pace, dining, and private camp buyouts. Select "Private Group / Custom" in the enquiry form below.
