03 · Pre-Departure FAQ

Questions Before You Turn the Key

Everything first-time self-drivers, solo travellers, and private groups ask before joining a Cairn & Axle convoy.

Ask a Road Captain
01 How do I book a road trip?
Pick any curated expedition on our Road Trips page or send your preferred dates via our Contact Dispatch. Within 24 hours, a dedicated Road Captain confirms open seats, shares the full route dossier, and sends a transparent booking link.
02 Are vehicles included in the package?
Both options are available on every departure: bring your own well-maintained vehicle in convoy, add a Cairn & Axle-prepped 4x4 rental SUV, or reserve a passenger seat in a shared expedition vehicle.
03 Can solo travellers join?
Yes — nearly 40% of our convoy guests join solo. We pair solo travellers comfortably into shared vehicles (or welcome solo self-drivers) and foster warm small-group camaraderie from Day One.
04 Are road trips suitable for beginners?
Absolutely. Each package clearly displays its driving difficulty rating. Beginners love our Coastal, Weekend, and Desert routes, while mountain passes include pre-trip hill-driving coaching and live VHF radio guidance.
05 What happens if the weather changes?
Every itinerary includes pre-vetted contingency routes and backup boutique stays. Our 24/7 operations desk monitors weather advisories and reroutes the convoy smoothly while prioritizing safety.
06 What is included in the package price?
Boutique stays, curated breakfasts and dinners, Lead and Sweep Road Captains, in-car VHF radios, regional permits, mechanical support, medical/oxygen backup on mountain routes, and printed route books.
07 What is the cancellation policy?
In our editable demo policy, cancellations 30+ days out receive a 90% refund or 100% future credit; 15–29 days out receive a 50% refund or free date transfer subject to seat availability.
08 Do you organise private group trips?
Yes — we build bespoke private convoys for families, photography collectives, car clubs, and corporate offsites tailored down to vehicle fleet, pace, dining, and private camp buyouts.
